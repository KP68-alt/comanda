Pricing Why restaurants switch

From menu to money, one scan.

Comanda builds a restaurant's menu with AI, puts it behind a QR code on every table, and runs the payment when guests pay from their phone.

  1. 1

    Describe the dishes

    Type them in or photograph the old paper menu. Comanda writes the descriptions, suggests prices and lays out the menu in minutes.

  2. 2

    Put a code on every table

    Guests scan, order and split the bill on their own phone, with no app to download. The code in the picture is real: point a camera at it.

  3. 3

    Take the payment through Comanda

    The money goes to the restaurant's bank account and Comanda keeps a small share. The owner sees which dishes sold, at what time, at which table.

A restaurant keeps about three cents of every dollar.

Food and labor take roughly two thirds of each sale, and card fees are the next big cost. The minutes between the last bite and a paid bill cost a table turn. Comanda works on both.
